§ 01 — About

A story rooted in place and belonging

Village to Valley is a book about the landscapes we carry within us — the village we come from, the valley we seek, and everything that transforms us in between.

It explores memory, movement, and the quiet courage it takes to leave one world and step into another. Told through personal narrative and vivid observation, it is a book about home — and about learning to feel at home in the unfamiliar.

This page is a living record of the book as it is being written. Check back to follow its progress.

"Between the village and the valley, there is a road that only you can walk — and walking it changes everything you thought you knew about where you come from."
— From the manuscript
